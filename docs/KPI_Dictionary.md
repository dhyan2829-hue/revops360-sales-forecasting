# RevOps360 KPI Dictionary & Metric Definitions Document

### 1. Win Rate (%)
- **Definition:** Percentage of closed deals successfully converted to revenue.
- **Formula:** `(Count of Closed Won Deals / Total Closed Deals [Won + Lost]) * 100`

### 2. Average Deal Size ($)
- **Definition:** Average value generated per winning deal.
- **Formula:** `Sum(Closed Won Value) / Count(Closed Won Deals)`

### 3. Sales Cycle Time (Days)
- **Definition:** Mean duration to advance an opportunity from creation to won.
- **Formula:** `Average(Close Date - Created Date)` for Closed Won opportunities.

### 4. Stage Conversion Rate (%)
- **Definition:** Transition rate of opportunities moving from stage N to stage N+1.
- **Formula:** `(Count of Deals Reaching Stage N+1 / Count of Deals Entering Stage N) * 100`

### 5. Weighted Pipeline Forecast ($)
- **Definition:** Probability-adjusted projected revenue based on opportunity stage.
- **Formula:** `Sum(Deal Amount * Stage Probability)`

### 6. Forecast Variance ($ / %)
- **Definition:** Deviation between actual closed revenue and weighted forecast.
- **Formula:** `Actual Closed Revenue - Weighted Forecast`
